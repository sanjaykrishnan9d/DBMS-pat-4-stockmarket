📐 DBMS Lab Calculation Methodology

1. Weighted Average Buy Price: When purchasing additional shares of stock $i$:
New_Avg_Price = ((Old_Qty * Old_Avg_Price) + (New_Qty * Purchase_Price)) / (Old_Qty + New_Qty)

2. Unrealized Profit / Loss:
Unrealized_P&L = (Shares_Owned * Current_Market_Price) - (Shares_Owned * Avg_Buy_Price)

3. Return Percentage:
Return_% = (Unrealized_P&L / Total_Cost) * 100
