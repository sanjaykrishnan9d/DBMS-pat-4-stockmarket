📚 DBMS Lab Accounting & Double-Counting Prevention

Prevention of Double-Counting: Traditional naive implementations count total purchase orders without subtracting cost of liquidated positions, resulting in inflated portfolios. Our Oracle database design isolates:

  • Total Historical Purchases: Absolute cash transferred into equities over account lifetime (SUM(TOTAL_AMOUNT) WHERE TYPE='BUY').
  • Current Cost Basis: Cost basis of shares currently retained in PORTFOLIO table.
  • Cost Basis of Sold Shares: Total Historical Purchases - Active Cost Basis.
  • Realized P&L: Total Sales Proceeds - Cost Basis of Sold Shares.
  • Unrealized P&L: Current Market Value of Active Portfolio - Active Cost Basis.
